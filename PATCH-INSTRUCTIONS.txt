Existing unpaid purchases are NOT automatically modified on startup.
Repair them separately using repair_existing_credits_v13.py:
1) Back up your Railway volume and take a DB snapshot (or download safely).

3) Verify exact purchase IDs and eligibility; do not assume they are consecutive.
4) Only when verified run with --purchase-ids ID1,ID2 --apply.
   Script creates a .bak snapshot before modification and protects against second application.

IMPORTANT: Do NOT upload the repair script as a public web route or run it automatically at startup.
Test on a copy of the database first. A user_id must be confirmed from the live DB; STU-0012 is not necessarily user_id=12.

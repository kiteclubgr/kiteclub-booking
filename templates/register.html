Kitesurfing Release of Liability, Waiver of Legal Rights and Assumption of Risk (English)

I fully understand that Kitesurfing / Kiteboarding and other kite sports are considered extreme sports due to their inherent and numerous risks.

Some risks may include: being lifted by the kite and dropped onto the ground or water; severe cuts caused by kite lines; unpredictable sea life; unknown underwater conditions and objects; being dragged by the kite; changes in weather conditions, including increasing wind and lightning; and equipment malfunction.

Kiteboarding poses risks of injury or even death. I understand that I am voluntarily participating and assuming the associated risks.

In the event of injury, the original school waiver addresses reliance on personal health insurance for medical expenses. It also states that participants may stop a lesson if they lack confidence in their instructor and may request a pro-rated or full refund.

This is a digital summary of the existing English paper waiver, not a replacement for its full legally reviewed text.
